## PSYCHEDELIC REFERENCES IN $DADRAPS RHYMES

Throughout the expanded lyrics, $DadRaps weaves references to various aspects of psychedelic culture and research from "The Shifting Landscape of Psychedelics":

1. **"McKenna brothers dropped that forbidden fruit"** - References Terence and Dennis McKenna's work with psychedelic plants and the "Stoned Ape" theory

2. **"The heroic dose in silent darkness"** - Direct reference to Terence McKenna's recommendation for consuming five dried grams of psilocybin mushrooms in silent darkness

3. **"Set and setting, that's the key"** - References Timothy Leary's concept about the importance of mindset and environment in shaping psychedelic experiences

4. **"Shulgin synthesized two hundred compounds"** - References Alexander Shulgin's work creating and testing over 230 psychoactive compounds

5. **"The DEA couldn't handle chemical truth"** - References the 1994 DEA raid on the Shulgins' laboratory after the publication of PiHKAL

6. **"Strassman found the spirit molecule inside"** - References Rick Strassman's pioneering DMT research in the 1990s and his book "DMT: The Spirit Molecule"

7. **"Vollenweider scanned the brain on mushroom power"** - References Franz Vollenweider's neuroimaging research with psilocybin

8. **"Saw the default mode network start to flower"** - References neuroimaging findings about how psychedelics affect brain network activity

9. **"The rave culture knew what academics found"** - References the connection between 1990s rave culture and the emerging scientific understanding of these substances

10. **"The 432 hertz they couldn't stand"** - References alternative frequency theories popular in psychedelic communities

## STYLISTIC ELEMENTS

The $DadRaps character combines several distinct hip-hop and electronic music styles that emerged in the 1990s:

1. **Trip-Hop Influences** - The slower, atmospheric quality with samples and a melancholic feel draws from Bristol-based artists like Massive Attack and Tricky

2. **Golden Age Hip-Hop Flow** - The cadence and rhyme structures borrow from early 90s conscious rap styles

3. **Glitch Aesthetics** - The intentional stutters, repeats, and digital artifacts reference both the emerging glitch music genre and the character's unstable digital nature

4. **Rave Culture References** - The collective consciousness themes and references to rhythmic sound connect to the communal aspects of 90s rave scenes

5. **Psychedelic Trance Elements** - The focus on frequency, vibration, and consciousness expansion connects to Goa/psytrance themes
